# Project SEO Rules

This workspace enforces strict guidelines on SEO keyphrase utilization and indexability.

## Rules
1. **Short-form & Long-form Keywords Integration**:
   - Always optimize metadata titles, descriptions, and page headings with a blend of short-form and long-form keywords.
   - Target terms include:
     - **Short-form**: `handloom saree`, `saree shop`, `pure silk mark`, `puri handloom`, `sambalpuri saree`, `bomkai saree`.
     - **Long-form**: `best saree shop in puri`, `authentic handloom sarees in puri`, `silk mark certified saree store in puri`, `buy sambalpuri silk sarees online`.
2. **Sitemap Dynamic Mapping**:
   - Every product page `/product/[id]` must be mapped in `src/app/sitemap.ts` to generate dynamic index listings.
